# Service and Maintenance

## Preventive Maintenance
Every robot should receive preventive maintenance every 6 months. Maintenance visits during the first year are free of charge. After the first year, maintenance is included in the annual service contract.

## Software Updates
Robot software is updated over the air once a month. Updates are installed automatically at night while the robot is charging, so service is not interrupted.

## On-site Service Response Time
In İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir and Eskişehir a technician arrives on site within 48 hours. In other cities the on-site response time is 72 hours. Customers can ask the assistant for the nearest service center in their city.

## Installation and Training
Installation and a half-day operator training are included in the purchase price of every robot. The installation team also creates the map of the site for Rehber R2, Taşıyıcı T5 and Temizlik C3.
