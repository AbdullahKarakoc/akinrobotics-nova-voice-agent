# Warranty and Returns Policy

## Standard Warranty
All Nova Robotik robots (Rehber R2, Taşıyıcı T5 and Temizlik C3) come with a 2-year standard warranty starting from the delivery date. The Eğitim E1 education kit has a 1-year warranty. Batteries are covered for 1 year on all products.

## Extended Warranty
Customers can extend the warranty of Rehber R2, Taşıyıcı T5 and Temizlik C3 up to a total of 5 years by purchasing an extended warranty package before the standard warranty expires.

## What Is Not Covered
The warranty does not cover physical damage caused by misuse, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, or modifications to the robot's software or hardware.

## Returns
Unused products in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Robots that have been installed and commissioned on site cannot be returned, but are covered by the warranty.

## Repairs
Warranty repairs are typically completed within 10 business days. If a repair takes longer, a replacement robot can be provided to enterprise customers.
